# Tablet Research: Photo Frame Use Case

## Recommendation

**Best price-to-quality: Samsung Galaxy Tab S7+** (12.4", Super AMOLED, 2020)
- Refurbished on refurbed.pl or used on Allegro: ~1,300–1,500 PLN
- 12-month warranty on refurbed.pl, 30-day return
- 12.4" AMOLED is the whole point — photos look vivid with true blacks
- Hardware (Snapdragon 865+) is more than enough for a slideshow kiosk
- Wide accessory availability — kickstand folio cases from ~50 PLN on Allegro

**Runner-up: Honor MagicPad 2** (12.3", OLED 144Hz, 2024)
- New, ~1,700–1,800 PLN with full warranty
- Newest hardware and the best display in this price range
- Worth the ~300 PLN premium over S7+ if buying new matters to you

**Budget pick: Samsung Tab S9 FE+** (12.4", LCD, 2023)
- ~1,200–1,600 PLN new
- No AMOLED, but a well-calibrated LCD at this size is genuinely good enough
- Choose this if the AMOLED premium doesn't feel worth it

## Tradeoffs

### AMOLED/OLED vs IPS LCD

OLED pixels emit their own light — black means the pixel is off, giving true black and infinite contrast. Photos look punchy and vivid. IPS LCD uses a backlight behind all pixels, so blacks are dark grey rather than true black, and contrast is limited (~1500:1).

That said, quality IPS panels (Apple Liquid Retina, Samsung FE series) have excellent color accuracy — often more natural and less oversaturated than OLED. Professional photographers frequently prefer IPS for color work. For a casual photo frame the difference is "wow factor" vs "accuracy."

**Burn-in risk:** OLED can develop permanent ghost images where static UI elements (clock, album name) stay in fixed positions for long daily use. IPS LCD has no burn-in. For a frame running 8+ hours a day, this is worth considering — ImmichFrame rotates photos which helps, but UI overlays are a risk over years.

Verdict: OLED wins on visual impact. Good IPS is not a bad choice.

### Refurbished vs New

Refurbished (refurbed.pl, Allegro) gives access to premium AMOLED models (S7+, S8+) at 40–50% below original retail. Risk is cosmetic wear and shorter remaining battery life. refurbed.pl units come with 12-month warranty and 30-day trial — a reasonable safety net.

New at similar price points means accepting LCD (FE series, Honor MagicPad 2 is the exception — new OLED at ~1,700 PLN).

### Size

All models in the >10" category are 11–12.6". For a table-top frame, 12.4" (S7+, S8+, S9 FE+, Honor MagicPad 2) is noticeably more frame-like than 11". The 10.5" Tab S6 is borderline — probably too small for this use case.

### Brand / Software

Samsung One UI is the most mature Android tablet experience. Long software support (4 OS upgrades on S series). Honor runs Android with MagicOS on top — less familiar but functional. Lenovo and Xiaomi are fine for a kiosk but One UI has better tablet multitasking (irrelevant here) and wider accessory availability.

### iPad

iPad Pro M4 (2024) has the best display of anything discussed — Tandem OLED, outstanding. But:
- Starts at ~4,000–5,000 PLN — overkill for a photo frame
- ImmichFrame has no native iOS app — requires a browser-based workaround
- Older/cheaper iPads (Air, standard) use IPS LCD, not OLED

### Standing on a Table

No tablet comes with a built-in kickstand. Options:
- **Kickstand folio case** (30–80 PLN, Allegro) — adjustable angle, portrait and landscape
- **Samsung Smart Book Cover** (~100–150 PLN) — official, clean integrated kickstand
- **Universal aluminum desk stand** (30–60 PLN) — holds the tablet naked, minimal look
